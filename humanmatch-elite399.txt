  THE TWO HALVES ARE NOT MEASURED EQUALLY, and this is where they part. The SWITCH menu is
  complete — every legal bench body — so the switch half is a FAIR reading of this engine. The
  ATTACK menu can only hold the moves the log had already revealed (8732 of 25284 clicks
  were not yet revealed), so the MOVE half is a LOWER BOUND: the engine was choosing between
  fewer attacks than the human could see. That is also why its top-1 is a switch far more often
  than the human's click was. Do not average the two halves into one number and call it a
  rating — the fair half is the weaker one, and the unfair half is the stronger one.

  the ranking still had to order); 0.05 is "a wash", 0.30+ is the engine saying the human
  threw value away. Read it beside the top-1 rate: agreement counts a tie-broken row as a
  miss, and this column says how much that miss was worth.
  The TWO "within 5%" columns normalise differently and are NOT interchangeable: the first
  is 5 % of the menu's own spread (bounded, comparable across turns, the headline), the
  second is the literal 5 % of the top line's SCORE, which is undefined when that score is
  near zero — the evaluator's units cross zero — so it is counted over fewer decisions.
